[[business logic vulnerabilities]]

- arises because of the design and assumptions by the developers for how the end user will use the product.
- bad assumptions may lead to:
	- inadequate validation of user input e.g.
		  assumptions that users will pass data by the conventional way, e.g. using a browser may lead to broken client side validation, easily bypassed by using interception proxy.
- common on overly complicated systems that even the developers don't fully understand them.
- to avoid them understand the whole system including functions and their purpose on the system because it might lead to this sort of vulnerabilities creeping in.
